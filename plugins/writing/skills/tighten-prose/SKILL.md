---
name: tighten-prose
description: Edit text to be clearer and more concise without changing its meaning or voice. Use when the user asks to tighten, trim, or sharpen writing.
---

Tighten the provided text. Preserve the author's meaning, facts, and voice — do not invent claims or change the argument.

Apply these passes:

1. Cut filler and hedging ("very", "really", "in order to", "it is worth noting that", "basically").
2. Prefer active voice and strong verbs over nominalizations ("decide" over "make a decision").
3. Remove redundant words and duplicated ideas; merge sentences that say the same thing.
4. Keep technical terms exact; never sacrifice precision for brevity.
5. Preserve formatting (lists, code, headings) and any domain-specific terminology.

Return the edited text. If the change is non-trivial, follow it with a short bullet list of the substantive edits you made so the author can review them.
